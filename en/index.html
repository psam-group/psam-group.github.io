PSAM-Group — Plant Stress Analysis & Modeling

Institute of Plant Physiology and Genetics, Bulgarian Academy of Sciences, Sofia, Bulgaria.

We investigate abiotic plant stress through photosynthesis, chlorophyll fluorescence, experimental biophysics, mathematical modeling and artificial intelligence.

Research areas

  • Drought stress physiology in plants
  • Nutrient deficiency analysis
  • Mathematical modeling of plant stress
  • AI and predictive analytics
